Task 6: Create a Strong Password and Evaluate Its Strength

Objective:
To understand what makes a password strong by testing passwords using an online password strength checker.

Tool Used:
PasswordMeter (https://passwordmeter.com)

Observations:
- Longer passwords are more secure.
- Using uppercase and lowercase letters increases strength.
- Numbers and special characters improve password security.
- Avoid using common words or personal information.

Best Practices:
- Use at least 12–16 characters.
- Mix uppercase, lowercase, numbers, and symbols.
- Avoid dictionary words.
- Use a different password for every account.
- Enable Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA).
- Store passwords in a password manager.

Conclusion:
Strong passwords reduce the risk of brute-force and dictionary attacks. Combining long passwords with unique characters and MFA provides better security.
